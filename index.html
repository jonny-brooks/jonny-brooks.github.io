About

Jonny Brooks is an ESRC-funded doctoral researcher pursuing a DPhil in International Development at the University of Oxford under the supervision of Professor Nikita Sud.

Alongside his research, Jonny works as Communications and Operations Manager at Action Village India, an NGO supporting projects in climate resilience, girls’ education, women’s economic empowerment and rural livelihoods.

Prior to joining the Oxford Department of International Development, Jonny held research positions at the LSE International Inequalities Institute, Law Business Research, Westminster Forum Projects and the University of Bristol.

Research

Jonny’s doctoral research explores the political ecology of aluminium production in Odisha, India.

His wider interests include natural-resource governance, environmental change, development and the relationships between industry, state institutions and local communities.
