About

Jonny Brooks is a DPhil candidate in International Development at the University of Oxford and a member of Wolfson College. Supervised by Professor Nikita Sud, he is supported by an ESRC Grand Union Doctoral Training Partnership Interdisciplinary Studentship.

Alongside his doctoral research, Jonny works as a Research Assistant at the LSE International Inequalities Institute and as Communications and Operations Manager at Action Village India, an international development charity focused on rural issues in India.

Jonny has experience across research, communications and operations. He has held roles at Law Business Research, Westminster Forum Projects and the University of Bristol, in addition to his current positions at LSE and Action Village India.

Research

Green Extractivism and the Government of Climate Transition: Frontier-Making, Accumulation and Dispossession

Jonny’s research explores the political ecology of development, seeking to understand the realities of environmental justice and the green transition. It examines relationships among institutions, power and local communities to understand how policy works in practice and how it could work more effectively.

His doctoral research focuses on aluminium production at two sites in Odisha, India: a proposed and contested bauxite mine in Koraput and an established bauxite mine in Kalahandi. It examines how extractive transition governance materialises in practice and how commitments to participation, development and justice are reshaped, constrained or normalised as extractive frontiers consolidate over time.

Jonny focuses on the “small print” of governance—including clearances, classifications, consent procedures and compensation rules—through which extraction is both normalised and contested. He uses a qualitative-led, mixed-methods design integrating spatial and administrative data, drawing on relationships developed through his ongoing work with Action Village India.
